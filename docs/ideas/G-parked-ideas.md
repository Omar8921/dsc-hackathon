# Parked Ideas (researched, not shortlisted)

Kept here for context, with the reason each one was parked.

## G1 — Mapping Amman's informal transit (coasters & service taxis) — "Digital Matatus for Amman"
- **Proof:** MIT/Columbia/U. Nairobi's **Digital Matatus** used phones to map 130+ informal matatu routes for 3.5M daily riders and turned them into GTFS **now in Google Maps**. ([MIT DUSP](https://dusp.mit.edu/projects/digital-matatus), [digitalmatatus.com](https://digitalmatatus.com/))
- **Jordan:** volunteers (Ma'an Nasel / Khutoutna) hand-mapped **76 bus/coaster/servees routes in 2015**. It's outdated, and the Amman Bus app covers only formal buses. ([Jordan Times](https://jordantimes.com/news/local/map-outlines-public-transport-routes-amman), [7iber](https://www.7iber.com/mapping-serveece-routes/))
- **Why parked:** the core asset is weeks of GPS data collection, which can't be shown in 11 hours. Transport is also a crowded area. **Great long-term idea.** An AI angle would be inferring routes/stops from crowdsourced rider GPS traces.

## G2 — Water: tank-depletion forecast / tanker price transparency
- Amman households get piped water ~1–1.5 days a week and depend on rooftop tanks. **Tankers supply ~15% of drinking water but 52% of urban water revenue**, at up to **23× the piped price**. ([The Conversation](https://theconversation.com/climate-crisis-sees-rise-in-illegal-water-markets-in-the-middle-east-220590))
- **Why parked:** the website lists water first, so it's the most crowded area. Tank prediction needs sensors. The tanker-price angle is interesting (the poor are priced out) but data is scarce. Revisit only if we find tanker price data.

## G3 — Rooftop-solar soiling (dust) cleaning optimiser
- Jordan studies: ~0.6–0.77% efficiency loss **per day** from dust. Optimal cleaning every 12–15 days. Cleaning every 2 weeks instead of every 3 months gives **+4% energy**. ([MDPI Sustainability 13/14/7636](https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/13/14/7636))
- **Why parked:** helps solar owners, a relatively well-off group, so it's weak on "who is left out". It also leans towards the economy sector.

## G4 — Takaful / National Aid Fund eligibility helper
- Exclusion issues are documented: an algorithm with 57 indicators, rural access barriers. ([HRW](https://www.hrw.org/news/2023/07/04/jordan-should-take-social-protection-all-way))
- **Why parked:** it's a "government-procedure chatbot" in disguise (crowded), politically sensitive, and the eligibility algorithm isn't public.

## G5 — Emergency addressing, sign language, appointment no-shows
- Jordan already has E9-1-1 with caller location. Sign language is crowded. No-show reduction lacks Jordanian data (KAUH study exists, 65k appointments). Parked.
