# Big, Obvious Jordan Problems: problems only, no solutions (research, 2026-10-09)

Why this file exists: **Impact on Jordan = 25%** of the score. Instead of starting from cool ideas, start from problems every Jordanian (and every judge) already knows are real. Then pick one where **one simple product** can make a measurable dent and **AI is genuinely needed**.

Each problem is tested against the team's five filters:
- **Big & obvious:** a judge nods without needing convincing.
- **Single-solution dent:** one product can move a real number, rather than needing national policy.
- **AI convincing:** AI does something a form or website can't.
- **Simple:** the solution would be obvious once stated.
- **Society sector:** fits `next.society()` rather than economy.

Ratings: ●●● strong · ●● medium · ● weak. *Our judgment; argue with it.*

## Health & wellbeing

### 1. Chronic disease is everywhere and much of it is unmanaged
- **Obesity 32.3%, overweight 60.7%** of adults (STEPS 2019, age-standardised). ([WHO EMRO nutrition profile](https://applications.emro.who.int/docs/WHOEMNUT296E-eng.pdf))
- **Diabetes ≈ 20.5% of adults (≈1.1M people, 2024); 18.6% of them undiagnosed.** ([IDF Diabetes Atlas](https://diabetesatlas.org/data-by-location/country/jordan/)) STEPS 2019: 20% of adults aged 45–69 have raised glucose. ([WHO EMRO](https://www.emro.who.int/jordan/news/results-of-jordan-national-stepwise-survey-steps-of-noncommunicable-diseases-and-their-risk-factors-2019.html))
- **Hypertension 22%; nearly half (47.8%) of them not taking medication.** (same STEPS source)
- NCDs are the leading cause of death. ([Jordan Times](https://jordantimes.com/news/local/noncommunicable-diseases-leading-cause-death-jordan-—-survey))
- **Where it breaks:** diagnosis happens, then patients drop off. There's no follow-up, no adherence, and no one checking between clinic visits.
- Big & obvious ●●● · Single-solution ●●● · AI ●●● · Simple ●●● · Society ●●●

### 2. Smoking: among the highest rates in the world
- **Men: 55–66% smoke** (66% STEPS 2019 for ages 18–69; 55.1% in a 2024 estimate). **Women 13–17%. Adults overall ≈35%.** Includes shisha. ([WHO FCTC Jordan report](https://extranet.who.int/fctcapps/sites/default/files/2023-04/Jordan_2020_WHOFCTCreport.pdf), [GSTHR](https://gsthr.org/countries/profile/jor/cigarettes/), [Jordan Times](https://jordantimes.com/news/local/national-survey-reveals-high-tobacco-use-among-jordanians-urges-stronger-anti-smoking))
- Not literally #1 globally (Indonesia's male rate is higher on a broader measure), but **far above** regional averages.
- **Where it breaks:** people want to quit but there's almost no accessible, continuous quit support, and cravings happen at 11 pm, not during clinic hours.
- Big & obvious ●●● · Single-solution ●●● · AI ●●● · Simple ●●● · Society ●●●

### 3. Overcrowded public emergency departments and violence against staff
- **~1.5M public ED visits a year** (9.16M over 2018–2023), **only 6.4% admitted.** ([Int J Emerg Med 2025](https://intjem.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12245-025-00952-x))
- **63.1% of Amman doctors faced violence in the past year**, mostly in EDs; **61% of patients don't know triage exists**. (See [ideas/M](ideas/M-ed-triage-copilot.md))
- Big & obvious ●●● · Single-solution ●● · AI ●●● · Simple ●● · Society ●●●

### 4. Mental-health care is close to non-existent for most people
- **~0.5–2 psychiatrists per 100,000** depending on the source and year (WHO-AIMS 2011: 1.09; MoH 2021: under 1). ([WHO-AIMS](https://www.ecoi.net/file_upload/1788_1308390842_mh-aims-report-jordan-jan-2011-en.pdf), [Frontiers 2019](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychiatry/articles/10.3389/fpsyt.2019.00556/full))
- **About a third of adolescents report moderate-to-severe depression** (34% of 2,349 aged 12–17; 32.4% of 784 in Amman). ([Duke Nursing](https://nursing.duke.edu/news/phd-alumna-dardas-silva-noonan-and-simmons-publish-article-public-health-nursing))
- **Where it breaks:** stigma, cost and almost no professionals.
- Big & obvious ●●● · Single-solution ●● · AI ●● (risky, and "mental-health chatbot" is on our crowded list) · Simple ●● · Society ●●●

### 5. Cancer found late (breast cancer as the clearest case)
- Breast-cancer **mammography participation reported below 10%** despite a national free programme (JBCP). Hospital studies say most cases present at an advanced stage. ([Dove 2020](https://www.dovepress.com/knowledge-attitude-and-practice-around-breast-cancer-and-mammography-s-peer-reviewed-fulltext-article-BCTT), [PMC10956363](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10956363/))
- Data is older and patchy.
- Big & obvious ●● · Single-solution ●● · AI ●● · Simple ●●● · Society ●●●

## Education & youth

### 6. Learning poverty: most children can't read properly by age 10
- **52% (pre-COVID) → over 60% now**, per the Education Minister. ([Jordan News](https://jordannews.jo/Section-109/News/Learning-poverty-surges-from-52-5-to-over-60-post-COVID-Mahafzah-34800))
- **PISA 2022 math: 361**, Jordan's lowest ever. ([OECD](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/pisa-2022-results-volume-i-and-ii-country-notes_ed6fbcc5-en/jordan_d1c865b3-en.html))
- **826 double-shift schools; 29.4% of public-school students in them.** ([Jordan Times](https://jordantimes.com/node/270364))
- **Where it breaks:** early-grade **Arabic reading** isn't caught and fixed in classes of 45+.
- Big & obvious ●●● · Single-solution ●● · AI ●●● · Simple ●● · Society ●●●

### 7. Unemployment, especially youth and women ⚠️ economy sector
- **Jordanians: 21.0% unemployed (Q2 2026).** ([Jordan News / DoS](https://www.jordannews.jo/Section-109/News/Jordanian-Unemployment-Rate-Falls-to-21-in-Q2-2026-54976))
- **Ages 15–34: 30%** (JLMPS 2025). **Female participation ≈15%**, among the lowest in the world. ([IGC/ERF brief](https://www.theigc.org/sites/default/files/2026-05/Assaad-Krafft-Abushehab-Policy-Brief-March-2026.pdf))
- Biggest problem in the country, but it's **the economy sector's own example**.
- Big & obvious ●●● · Single-solution ● · AI ●● · Simple ● · Society ● (mentor check)

## City, environment & resources

### 8. Water scarcity
- **~60 m³ per person per year**, among the lowest in the world (500 = absolute scarcity line). **~50% non-revenue water.** ([Jordan Times](https://jordantimes.com/news/local/public-urged-ration-water-storage-dams-stands-15), [National Water Strategy](https://agwkp.unescwa.org/sites/default/files/2023-10/National_Water_Strategy_2023-2040_Summary-English_-ver2-%20Jordan.pdf))
- Piped supply ~1–1.5 days a week; tanker water costs up to 23× more (see round-1 docs).
- Big & obvious ●●● · Single-solution ● (losses are network-side) · AI ●● · Simple ●● · Society ●●● · ⚠️ the most crowded area at this hackathon

### 9. Traffic congestion and car dependency
- **Congestion costs JD 1–1.5 billion a year** (GAM estimate / transport model). ([Jordan News](https://www.jordannews.jo/Section-106/Features/Traffic-congestion-losses-amount-to-JD1-5-billion-annually-963))
- **Public transport ≈13% of trips in Amman** (vs 58% in Istanbul); vehicles growing ~5.5%/yr; 7M car trips a day in Amman. ([CODATU](https://www.codatu.org/en/planning-sustainable-mobility-in-the-amman-metropolitan-area-jordan/))
- Big & obvious ●●● · Single-solution ● · AI ●● · Simple ● · Society ●●●

### 10. Road deaths, mostly pedestrians
- **187,213 accidents, 510 deaths (2025)**; **pedestrians = 42.4% of deaths (2024).** (See [sources](sources.md), round 1/2)
- Big & obvious ●●● · Single-solution ●● · AI ●● · Simple ●● · Society ●●●

### 11. Waste: almost nothing recycled
- **Amman: ~3,200–4,000 tonnes a day** to Al-Ghabawi (55% of Jordan's waste), **recycling 7–10%**, generation growing >5%/yr; **50% of it organic.** ([USAID/Edama factsheet](https://edama.jo/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/3-Factsheet_Recycling_in_Jordan.pdf), [IDOM](https://idom.com/en/?p=45911), [African Clean Cities](https://www.africancleancities.org/sites/default/files/2024-11/IMPROVING_DISPOSAL_SITE_OPERATION_Amman%20Jordan.pdf))
- Big & obvious ●● · Single-solution ● · AI ● · Simple ●● · Society ●●● · ⚠️ "recycling classifier" is on the crowded list

## Social safety

### 12. Drugs (Captagon, synthetics)
- **25,776 drug cases in 2025** (NCHR) / 22,031 (PSD). Down from 2024, but still about 60 a day. ([The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/world/2026/09/16/jordan-sees-34-pct-drop-in-drug-related-crimes-in-2025), [Jordan News](https://www.jordannews.jo/Section-109/News/Drug-Trafficking-Crimes-in-Jordan-Declined-by-18-69-Last-Year-50900))
- Big & obvious ●●● · Single-solution ● · AI ● · Simple ● · Society ●●● (sensitive)

### 13. Domestic violence (heavily under-reported)
- Complaints to the Family Protection Department rose **11,000 (2020) → ~16,000 (2021)**; officials say the data is "the shallow depth of a deeply troubled sea". No 2025 figures found. ([Jordan News](https://www.jordannews.jo/Section-106/Features/Complaints-of-domestic-violence-on-the-rise-19925))
- Big & obvious ●● · Single-solution ● · AI ● · Simple ● · Society ●●● (sensitive)

### 14. Online scams and cybercrime
- **29,389 cybercrime court cases (2025)**, plus daily WhatsApp scam waves and AI voice-clone warnings. (See [ideas/K](ideas/K-scam-message-checker.md))
- Big & obvious ●●● · Single-solution ●● · AI ●●● · Simple ●●● · Society ●● (overlaps economy)

### 15. Poverty and cost of living (context, not a target)
- **35%** below the World Bank's $7.9/day line (Atlas of SDGs 2023); 16% on the last official national line (2017–18). No recent official estimate. ([Jordan News](https://jordannews.jo/Section-109/News/More-than-one-third-of-Jordanians-live-below-poverty-line-report-finds-29697), [ERF](https://erf.org.eg/app/uploads/2024/12/1734943008_620_498794_pb143.pdf))
- Too broad for one product, but it's the **lens** for "who is left out" in every other problem.
