# Hackathon Brief — AI Quest 2026 (Future Jordan Hackathon)

Source: https://aiquest2026.site/#/hack (captured 2026-10-09, Day 1)

## Logistics
- **Dates:** 9–10 October 2026, Al Hussein Technical University (HTU). Build floor: Orange Village (overflow W-002 / W-003).
- **Working time:** roughly **11 working hours** across both days.
- **Submission deadline:** **1:00 PM on Day 2 (10 Oct)** via the Submit page.
- **Presentations:** from 1:30 PM Day 2 in W-002 / W-003. **7 min presentation + demo, then 3 min Q&A.**
- Team size 2–5, 30 teams. Organiser contact: Raneem Al-Khanji, DSC President.

## Our sector: `next.society()` — Smart Society & Public Services
> Something that makes a real service in Jordan faster, clearer, or reachable by more people than it is today. Expect the panel to ask **who is currently left out, and whether your app actually reaches them.**

Sub-areas: smart cities, public services, transport & mobility, environment & sustainability, accessibility & inclusion, safety & emergency response, education & learning, urban planning & infrastructure, community engagement.

Key question the panel asks this sector: **"Who does this reach that the current service does not?"**

## Suggested problem areas (we are NOT required to use them)
| Area | Problem | Directions |
|---|---|---|
| Water & resources | Water delivered on rotation; hard to plan/save | Usage prediction, leak detection, saving tips, distribution |
| Transport & mobility | Hard to plan, little real-time info | Wait times, route planning, crowding, safety |
| Government services | Unclear documents/offices/steps, repeat visits | Arabic assistants, form help, navigation |
| Access to healthcare | Finding right care at right place/time | Care navigation, appointments, reminders |
| Accessibility & inclusion | Hard for elderly, disabled, low literacy | Voice-first, simplified Arabic, assistive nav |
| Environment & sustainability | Waste, air, heat, energy | Recycling, energy saving, pollution/heat awareness |
| Safety & emergency | Floods, fires, accidents need faster info | Early warning, incident reporting, guidance |
| Education & learning | Different paces, uneven support | Tutoring, Arabic content, study support |
| Community & social impact | Needs and help hard to connect | Matching, community reporting |

## Judging (3 judges per panel: domain, AI/technical, business)
| Criterion | Weight | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Impact on Jordan | **25%** | Real problem, clear group of people in Jordan who benefit |
| Innovation & Creativity | **20%** | Fresh angle, or AI used where it truly adds value |
| Feasibility & Scalability | **20%** | Realistic path to real users, data and cost |
| Technical Implementation | **15%** | Working demo; **honesty about what is mocked** |
| UX & Design | **10%** | Usable by intended users, **including in Arabic** |
| Presentation & Pitch | **10%** | Problem, demo, impact, next step — within time |

## Rules that constrain idea choice
- **Data rules:** public data, data collected on the day, and clearly labelled synthetic data are fine. **No real personal data without consent.**
- Start from a problem, not a technology. Stay close to the sector (check with a mentor if crossing into economy).
- "A small idea that works live beats a big one that only exists in slides."
- Own-idea checklist: *Who exactly has this problem in Jordan? What does it cost them (time/money/wellbeing)? What can we show working by the deadline?*

## Deliverables
1. Functional prototype / MVP
2. Source code repository (this repo)
3. Pitch deck
4. Technical documentation — README explaining how to run it
5. Live demonstration
