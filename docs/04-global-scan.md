# Global Scan — Proven Solutions Abroad → Jordan Gaps (Round 2)

Round 1 was too Jordan-first: we started from Jordanian problems. Round 2 flips it: **start from things that demonstrably work somewhere in the world, then check whether Jordan has the same gap.** An idea qualifies only if the solution is *in production* abroad (not just research) and the Jordanian gap is backed by a number.

## Patterns worth noting
1. **Gulf e-government is the richest source** of Jordan-transferable ideas. Many Jordanians have lived in KSA/UAE and know these apps, so judges understand them instantly ("it's Najm for Jordan") and the pitch gets easier.
2. **Look for "the last physical step" in an already-digital service.** E-Kroka is digital but needs an officer present; Sanad is digital but some steps still need a visit. Removing the last physical step is high-impact and integrates with something that exists, which helps feasibility.
3. **Follow the money:** ideas where an existing institution is *losing money* (insurers on MTPL) have a natural payer. That answers the business judge.
