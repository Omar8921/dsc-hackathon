# Hackathon Brief: AI Quest 2026 (Future Jordan Hackathon)

Source: https://aiquest2026.site/#/hack (captured 2026-10-09, Day 1)

## Logistics
- **Dates:** 9–10 October 2026, Al Hussein Technical University (HTU). Build floor: Orange Village (overflow W-002 / W-003).
- **Working time:** roughly **11 working hours** across both days.
- **Submission deadline:** **1:00 PM on Day 2 (10 Oct)**, via the Submit page.
- **Presentations:** from 1:30 PM on Day 2 in W-002 / W-003. **7 min presentation + demo, then 3 min Q&A.**
- Team size 2–5, 30 teams. Organiser contact: Raneem Al-Khanji, DSC President.

## Our sector: `next.society()` (Smart Society & Public Services)
> Something that makes a real service in Jordan faster, clearer, or reachable by more people than it is today. Expect the panel to ask **who is currently left out, and whether your app actually reaches them.**

Sub-areas: smart cities, public services, transport & mobility, environment & sustainability, accessibility & inclusion, safety & emergency response, education & learning, urban planning & infrastructure, community engagement.

The panel's key question for this sector: **"Who does this reach that the current service does not?"**

## Judging (3 judges per panel: domain, AI/technical, business)
| Criterion | Weight | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Impact on Jordan | **25%** | A real problem, and a clear group of people in Jordan who benefit |
| Innovation & Creativity | **20%** | A fresh angle, or AI used where it truly adds value |
| Feasibility & Scalability | **20%** | A realistic path to real users, data and cost |
| Technical Implementation | **15%** | A working demo, and **honesty about what is mocked** |
| UX & Design | **10%** | Usable by the intended users, **including in Arabic** |
| Presentation & Pitch | **10%** | Problem, demo, impact, next step, all within time |

How to score well on each: [01-judging-playbook.md](01-judging-playbook.md).

## Rules
- **Data:** public data, data collected on the day, and clearly labelled synthetic data are fine. **No real personal data without consent.**
- Start from a problem, not a technology. Stay close to the sector (check with a mentor if something crosses into another sector).
- "A small idea that works live beats a big one that only exists in slides."
- Checklist the organisers give: *Who exactly has this problem in Jordan? What does it cost them (time, money, wellbeing)? What can we show working by the deadline?*

## Deliverables
1. A functional prototype / MVP
2. A source code repository
3. A pitch deck
4. Technical documentation: a README explaining how to run it
5. A live demonstration
